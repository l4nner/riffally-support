Quick checks

  • Metronome: confirm the BPM, sound, work/rest settings, and device volume.
  • Scales: choose a root and scale, then use the pattern and playback controls.
  • Tuner: allow microphone access, play one clear string at a time, and check the selected tuning and reference pitch.
  • Harmony: use Atlas to find a chord, Shape to change its grip, Path for voice leading, and Morph for one-note changes.
  • Ear: listen once without guessing, then connect what you hear to the matching interval or fretboard position.
  • Jam: choose a musical setting, confirm the tempo and key, then begin with a short focused session.
  • Practice: set the time you have and the skill you want to improve, then follow the session one step at a time.
  • Slowdown: try the included track first, set A and B around a difficult phrase, reduce Speed, enable Loop, and use the tempo ladder to work back toward 100%.
  • Loop: choose a tempo and bar count, tap Start, and begin playing after four count-in clicks. Follow the iPhone click and beat counter while recording.

RiffAlly Loop

Loop records your own playing for practice. Set 40–240 BPM, or use Tap tempo. Choose 1–64 bars in 4/4 before recording to finish automatically. With Choose afterward, tap Stop after your phrase, then select how many complete bars to keep from the beginning. You can shorten or restore the selection while the current take remains available.

Recording always uses the iPhone microphone and speaker. Four audible clicks count you in; clicks continue during recording and may become part of your loop. Only completed-loop playback uses a selected compatible Bluetooth media speaker. Check the Output label and select the desired speaker in Control Center if needed. Without an external output, playback uses the iPhone speaker. Wired headphones and AirPlay are not supported in this version.

Continuous Loop repeats without an intentional rest. Call and Response deliberately leaves an equal-length space after each repetition. Loop is a foreground, session-based tool with no saved-loop library, export, overdubbing, or recovery after relaunch. New loop and Clear loop discard the current take. Allow microphone access in Settings if recording is unavailable.

For a Loop timing or routing issue, include the BPM, bar count, practice mode, and speaker model with your report. Recordings are not sent to RiffAlly automatically.

Optional tips
