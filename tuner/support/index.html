Support

Need help, want to report a problem, or have an idea for RiffAlly Tuner?

Quick help

  • Allow microphone access when iOS asks. The tuner begins listening automatically.
  • Play one clear, sustained note and keep the instrument near the iPhone.
  • Reduce background noise and mute neighboring strings if the note will not settle.
  • Use Guide for standard six-string guitar tuning, or Precision for a larger reference workspace.
  • Check that Reference Pitch is A = 440.0 Hz unless your ensemble uses another standard.

Microphone troubleshooting

If the app cannot listen, open iPhone Settings, choose Privacy & Security, then Microphone, and enable RiffAlly Tuner. The app analyzes microphone input live and does not record or save audio.
