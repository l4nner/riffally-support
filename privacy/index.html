RiffAlly Metronome Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

RiffAlly Metronome does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your metronome and practice interval settings locally on your device so they remain available the next time you open the app. This information is not transmitted off your device.

Accounts, analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party software development kits.

Network access

The metronome and practice features work offline. If you choose the Privacy & Support link, iOS opens this website in your browser; your browser and website host may process standard connection information under their own policies.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before those changes are released.

Contact

For privacy questions, email l4nner@gmail.com or visit RiffAlly Support.
