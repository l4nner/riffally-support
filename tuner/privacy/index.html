RIFFALLY TUNER

Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

RiffAlly Tuner does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Microphone and audio

RiffAlly Tuner requires microphone access to identify the pitch of an instrument. Microphone audio is analyzed live on the device. The app does not record, store, transmit, or share microphone audio.

Information stored on your device

The app stores limited preferences and counters locally, such as whether the in-app guide has been seen and whether an Apple rating prompt may be appropriate. This information is not transmitted to RiffAlly or any third party. Deleting the app removes it.

Accounts, analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party software development kits.

Optional purchases

RiffAlly Tuner can offer completely optional tips that do not unlock features. Apple processes these purchases under Apple's privacy policy. RiffAlly Tuner does not receive or store payment-card details.

Network access

Core tuning features work offline. Network access may occur when iOS loads optional tip products from Apple or when you choose an external App Store, support, privacy, sharing, or feedback link.

Children

Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the new behavior is released.
