Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026

RiffAlly does not collect, sell, share, or track personal data.

Information stored on your device

RiffAlly apps may remember settings, practice choices, favorites, recent selections, calibration, tunings, or similar preferences in local app storage. RiffAlly does not receive this information. Deleting an app removes its local information.

Microphone and audio

RiffAlly Tuner uses microphone input only while tuning. Pitch analysis happens on the device; microphone audio is not recorded, stored, or transmitted by RiffAlly. Metronome, Scales, and Harmony do not require microphone access. Their musical audio is generated on the device.

Accounts, analytics, advertising, and tracking

The apps have no RiffAlly accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Optional tips

RiffAlly apps may offer optional consumable tips through Apple’s StoreKit system. Apple processes purchases and purchase history under Apple’s privacy policy. RiffAlly receives no payment-card details, and tips do not unlock app features.

External actions

Core music features work offline. If you choose an App Store link, rating, sharing, feedback, support, or privacy action, iOS opens the relevant Apple or web service. Those services and this website’s host may process standard connection information under their own policies.

Children

RiffAlly does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If RiffAlly’s data practices change, this policy and the relevant App Store privacy information will be updated before those changes are released.
